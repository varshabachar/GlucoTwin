A CGM tells you your glucose crossed 140. It tells you at the moment it happens.

GlucoTwin warns two hours earlier — and says what would change it. It fuses a FHIR
health record with CGM, wearable and meal data into a physiological twin per patient,
then learns that patient meal by meal: predict the peak before they eat, compare with
what the sensor recorded, re-fit.

Across 431 real meals it cut the error in the predicted peak by 22%. And for one
patient the alarm had already called stable: rice first, projected 146 — dal and sabzi
first, 135. Same food, same afternoon, the difference between crossing the line and not.

Built for the Happiest Health Digital Twin Challenge 2026. Open data, leave-one-subject-out,
and a script that proves the twin never sees the future.
